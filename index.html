The Story

Built from inside the injury

Kevin wrestled at the University of Iowa under Dan Gable, then spent 38 years as a physical therapist: sports medicine, industrial rehab, geriatrics, fall prevention. In 2006 he started Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. He's now a 3rd-degree black belt who has won Masters divisions at Worlds and Pan Ams.

Persistent Performance exists to turn that recovery, clinical and personal, into a system anyone can use: how to train around an aging or replaced joint, how to come back after surgery, and how to keep training for decades instead of quitting after the first serious injury.

"I have two artificial hips and I still train."
